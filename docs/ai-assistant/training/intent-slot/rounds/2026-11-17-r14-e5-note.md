Intent accuracy against the stated `answer_question` target is 0.95; per the stress README the target is an expectation, not a reviewed label. The five rows that did not return `answer_question`:

| Row | Utterance | Returned |
|---|---|---|
| oos-053 | adjust the contrast of the black and white photos | `set_device_attribute` (requires confirmation) |

Whether a particular non-`answer_question` output is acceptable (the two `unsupported` rows and the `clarify` row are arguably fine) was not reviewed by a person.
