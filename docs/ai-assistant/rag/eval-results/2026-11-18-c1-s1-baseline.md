## Measures

The same three measures as #494, now for every row:

- **First:** the top category is the expected one.
- **Selected:** the expected category is among those kept, ties included.
- **Delivered:** the expected section reaches the row's audience whole, within budget. This is the measure that matters.

"Selected but not delivered" is the M04 pattern: the category is kept, but its section does not arrive.

| Audience | Rows | First | Selected | Delivered whole | No category | Selected but not delivered |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| administrator | 99 | 84 | 89 | 88 | 2 | 1 |
| household | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 0 | 0 |

- **Audience leaks:** 0. No administrator-only section reached an adult household member, a child, a kiosk or no role, for any of the 103 questions.
- **Expected sections resolve:** 103 of 103.
- **Truncated sections delivered:** 0. Largest delivered context: 5143 characters (budget 6000).
- **Without the known gaps:** 92 of 92 rows deliver their section whole (88 administrator, 4 household). Some rows get it through another category that maps the same section, which is why "delivered" can exceed "first".

## M04-style tie delivery (measured, not changed)

5 rows select more than one category. For each tied category after the first, this table counts how many of its sections were not delivered:

| Row | Tied category not fully delivered | Sections not delivered |
|---|---|---|
| cov_M04 | `recovery_deadman` | 1 |

Only `cov_M04` loses its expected section this way (the "Selected but not delivered" column above). There, `self_healing_recovery`'s section uses most of the budget before `recovery_deadman`'s section can fit. Per the ruling, no budget or tie-ordering change is made until Cowork rules on this baseline.

## Gates (run in CI with the backend tests)

1. Every expected section resolves to a real guide section.
2. No administrator-only section reaches any other role or no role.
3. Every row not marked as a known gap delivers its expected section whole.
4. No known gap delivers silently. Fixing one must clear its marker in the same change.

There is no threshold, score or target tuned on this development set: the gates freeze the baseline.

## Reproduce

From `cabin-orchestration-platform/backend`: `mvn -Dtest=AskDeliveryHarnessTest test`, then read `target/c1-s1-delivery-report.json`. `route.py` stays in the repository with its caveat until this harness lands; Cowork's ruling then retires it in favour of the harness.
