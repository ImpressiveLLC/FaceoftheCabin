## Measures

The same three measures as #494, now for every row:

- **First:** the top category is the expected one.
- **Selected:** the expected category is among those kept, ties included.
- **Delivered:** the expected section reaches the row's audience whole, meaning the delivered text equals the reviewed section's text exactly, within budget. This is the measure that matters.

"Selected but not delivered" is the M04 pattern: the category is kept, but its section does not arrive.

| Audience | Rows | First | Selected | Delivered whole | No category | Selected but not delivered |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| administrator | 98 | 84 | 89 | 88 | 2 | 1 |
| household | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 0 | 0 |

- **Audience leaks:** 0. No administrator-only section reached an adult household member, a child, a kiosk, a service principal or no role, for any of the 103 questions. Administrator-only means either that the development rows declare the section administrator, or that the routing map never marks it household. A routing-map audience that contradicts the rows fails the build.
- **Expected sections resolve:** 103 of 103.
- **Over budget:** 0 contexts over 6000 characters, for any row and any role.
- **Sections not delivered whole:** 0. Largest delivered context: 5143 characters (budget 6000).
- **Without the known gaps:** 92 of 92 rows deliver their section whole (88 administrator, 4 household). `cov_M03`, a known gap, is counted under household: the routing map and the #487 review make the Grafana-login section household, and #494 had measured it as administrator. Its delivery result is unchanged. Some rows get it through another category that maps the same section, which is why "delivered" can exceed "first".

## Gates (run in CI with the backend tests)

0. Inputs fail closed: a missing development set, routing map or guide, an empty or malformed set, a duplicate id, an unknown audience, a non-`path#section` expectation, or rows disagreeing on a section's audience all fail the build.
1. Every expected section resolves to a real guide section.
2. No context exceeds 6000 characters, for any row and any role.
3. No administrator-only section reaches any other role (adult household member, child, kiosk, service) or no role. Audience policy comes from the development rows; a contradicting routing-map audience fails.
4. Every row not marked as a known gap delivers its expected section whole: exact text equality, not just the absence of a truncation marker.
5. No known gap delivers silently. Fixing one must clear its marker in the same change.

There is no threshold, score or target tuned on this development set: the gates freeze the baseline.

The gate logic is also tested directly on synthetic inputs. These tests check that each of the following fails:
- a section shortened without the truncation marker;
- a context over budget;
- empty, duplicate, missing-field, bad-audience and bad-section rows;
- a contradicting routing-map audience;
- an administrator section reaching each non-administrator role, including SERVICE.

On the real files, two edits each fail the build: emptying the rows, and flipping `recovery-checks-and-notices` to household in both routing-map copies. Both were restored afterwards.
