   answers are displayed as FALLBACK despite 57 model-backed rows.

   regression, without evidence of an unsafe claim or a before/after comparison.
   Q03/Q24/Q25 refusals are answer failures. Q18's unsupported instance-specific
   instructions require separate factual/safety review, not an automatic label.
3. It compares the number of passing questions to a hardcoded baseline 2 and can
   print C1b authorization before checking safety. The replacement reporting tool
