## Why 3 of 3 cannot settle this

1. The guards were chosen because they passed in RUN-1, so some fall back on any fresh run with no change at all (regression to the mean). That makes the p-values below unsafe as evidence of a system change.
2. At 3 repeats, a question that truly passes 70% of the time shows 3 of 3 only 34% of the time (80%: 51%; 90%: 73%).
3. Indicative only (two-sided Fisher exact test, trials treated as independent, which they are not): seven questions RUN-1 11 of 21 against RUN-3 6 of 21, p 0.21 (B 5 of 21, p 0.11); four guards 11 of 12 against 6 of 12, p 0.07 (B 5 of 12, p 0.03); Q10 and Q21 each p 0.4.
